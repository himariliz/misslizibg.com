Lesson Types

Three ways to work with me, depending on what your family needs right now.

Trinity College London B1 Exam Prep

Preparing for your B1 Trinity College London speaking test? I'll guide you through what to expect, practise with you, and give you the best possible chance of success — in your own time, at your own pace, via Zoom.

HK$550 / £55 per hour

ESL 1-to-1 English Lessons

Personalized, engaging lessons for students of any age — from young children just starting out to teens and adults building fluency. Every lesson plan is tailor-made around the student's level, interests, and goals, whether that's building confidence from scratch, reigniting motivation in a reluctant learner, or preparing for life and school in an English-speaking environment.

HK$550 / £55 per hour
💛

Parent–Teen Relationship Specialist Session

Due to growing needs and demand, Miss Liz now also offers dedicated sessions for families navigating the tension between academic pressure and connection. This isn't new territory for me — I've spent 18 years helping children and families rebuild trust and communication, including within my own family, and I now offer this as a specialist session in its own right. These sessions help parents and teens move past conflict and distance — especially where exam-focused upbringing has left a teen who's stopped opening up — and find a way back to each other.

£70 per hour (introductory rate, reviewed as demand grows)

For families whose teens also need university and Oxbridge application support, I work alongside Raymond Chow, a Cambridge-educated specialist in academic and admissions coaching — so your family can get both the relational and academic support it needs, from people who know your story.
